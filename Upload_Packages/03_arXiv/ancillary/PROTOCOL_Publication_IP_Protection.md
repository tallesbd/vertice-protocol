**Founder & Chief Architect, VÉRTICE Protocol**  
**Conceived:** May 2022

### Strategic Objective

To establish, through multiple, mutually reinforcing, timestamped public disclosures, irrefutable prior art and intellectual provenance for the core mathematical primitives, ontology, and architectural framework of the VÉRTICE Protocol — prior to any material commercial engagement or partnership.

This protocol transforms the documentation from internal intellectual work into a globally recognized, legally robust asset that underpins both the sovereignty of the standard and the personal attribution of its founder.

### Mandatory Publication Sequence (in Order of Execution)

**Phase 1 — Cryptographic & Version Control Timestamp**
- Create a signed Git repository with GPG-signed commits.
- Tag the complete snapshot with a clear message containing the UTC datetime.
- Generate a SHA-256 hash of the entire documentation package and commit it.

**Phase 2 — Blockchain Timestamp (OpenTimestamps)**
- Submit the root hash of the documentation package to the OpenTimestamps service.
- This embeds the existence proof directly into the Bitcoin blockchain — the most immutable public timestamp available.

**Phase 3 — Academic Prior Art (arXiv)**
- Submit the core mathematical documents (category-theoretic ontology, formal definitions of the ten primitives, Probabilistic Performance Surfaces, and Decision Friction Entropy Index) as preprints on arXiv.
- Recommended categories: math.CT (Category Theory), q-fin.GN (General Finance), stat.AP (Applications).
- The submission timestamp on arXiv constitutes strong prior art in academic, regulatory, and legal contexts worldwide.

**Phase 4 — Persistent Identifier & Institutional Repository (Zenodo)**
- Upload the complete, versioned package (including code, mathematical specifications, and this protocol) to Zenodo.
- Zenodo automatically mints a DOI and provides a citable, timestamped record with long-term preservation guarantees.

**Phase 5 — Institutional & Personal Attribution Anchors**
- Register/update the work on the founder’s ORCID profile (https://orcid.org/0009-0003-7961-6966).
- Publish a canonical version on the founder’s professional site (https://veriquantum.org).
- Immediately capture the public page via the Wayback Machine (archive.org) for an independent, third-party timestamp.

### Governance Note on Jurisdiction

The publication sequence above is deliberately jurisdiction-agnostic and relies on globally recognized, decentralized, and academic mechanisms. This approach maximizes the strength of the prior art claim irrespective of the final chosen legal domicile for the sovereign foundation (recommended hybrid: Delaware implementation vehicle + Swiss/Singapore foundation for the primitives).

### Execution Discipline

No substantive commercial discussion, pilot, or licensing conversation should occur until at minimum Phases 1–4 have been completed and documented. The existence of timestamped public records fundamentally alters the negotiating posture from "proposing a new standard" to "offering access to an already-established, publicly proven foundational layer."

This is not a procedural formality. It is a core element of the protocol’s strategic architecture.
